Cultural institution

Sustaining South Bay's Music Culture

South Bay Music convenes ensembles, performances, and civic musical life across the region. Patron support helps keep that public culture visible, local, and alive from season to season.

Become a Patron Explore Ensembles

Mission

A regional music life held in common.

South Bay Music exists to sustain a durable local culture of rehearsal, performance, learning, and listening. It serves the region not only by presenting concerts, but by giving musicians and audiences a place to belong to the sound of their own community.

Community impact

Music as public life, not background.

The institution's value is cultural, civic, and intergenerational. Patrons help keep musical participation rooted in place.

Regional performances and ensemble activity throughout the year
Open entry points for players, listeners, and supporters
Shared rehearsal rooms, concert halls, and civic gathering spaces
Local arts culture preserved through recurring patron support
