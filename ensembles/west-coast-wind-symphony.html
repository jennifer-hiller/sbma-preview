Program overview

An American program with scale, sweep, and breadth.

To mark 250 years since the founding of the United States, the program moves from fanfare to Broadway, from Carnegie Hall to one of the wind repertoire’s most searching symphonic works.

  • Summon the Heroes

    John Williams opens the evening with a bright ceremonial fanfare.

  • Symphonic Dances from West Side Story

    Leonard Bernstein brings theatrical energy and rhythmic edge to the center of the program.

  • Rhapsody in Blue

    George Gershwin carries the audience from Broadway to Carnegie Hall with one of America’s defining concert works.

  • Symphony No. 4

    David Maslanka’s tragic and beautiful symphony anchors the evening with hymn material, spiritual depth, and a Lincoln-inspired emotional arc.
