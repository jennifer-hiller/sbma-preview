description: >+
  Firsts for Band celebrates landmark moments in music written for band—works
  that were firsts in their own right and helped shape the repertoire we know
  today. From the first true symphony for wind band, to the first major concert
  work for band, to the groundbreaking decision to compose a video game score
  for band, each piece on this program represents a pivotal beginning. The
  evening also honors tradition with one of John Philip Sousa’s earliest
  marches, reminding us where the band legacy began. Together, these works trace
  a musical journey of innovation, courage, and “first steps” that continue to
  define the band experience. 




  Steven Allen Fox, Music Director/Conductor

  Matthew Visk, Assistant Conductor

  Symphony for Band by Persichetti


  Fanfare & Allegro by Williams


  Banner Saga by Wintory
